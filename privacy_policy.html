Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Interest Calculator is developed as an offline utility tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

1. Zero Data Collection

Interest Calculator does not collect, record, store, or transmit any personal information, financial data, device identifiers, or analytics.

All calculations (including Principal Amount, Interest Rates, Calendar Dates, and Durations) are performed strictly locally on your device in real-time memory and are cleared when you reset or close the application.

2. Offline Operation & No Network Permissions

The application operates 100% offline. It does not request or require Android internet access permissions (android.permission.INTERNET), ensuring that no information can leave your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Interest Calculator contains:

  • No third-party SDKs or analytics trackers.
  • No advertisements.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No social logins or registrations.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer directly through the application's repository or Google Play store listing.
